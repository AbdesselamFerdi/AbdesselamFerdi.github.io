Biography

I received my PhD in Biomedical Engineering from Constantine 1 University in Algeria in January 2025. During my PhD, I worked as a researcher at Université Paris-Est Créteil in France. In 2025, I began a postdoctoral position at the University of Tartu in Estonia. My research focuses on developing interpretable and efficient medical AI methods for clinical applications. I am currently open to academic and industry opportunities.
